Fix the funnel before you raise the budget

When results stall, the instinct is to spend more. But a funnel that loses most people at the landing page or the first reply will lose more people at a higher budget.

Walk the journey as a customer: the ad, the page on a phone, the first message, the follow-up. Note where you would give up.

Then fix the biggest drop first. Often it is a slow reply, not a creative problem.
