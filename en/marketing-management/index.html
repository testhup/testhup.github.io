What monthly management covers

Growth strategy and planning

Positioning, offers, channel mix and a plan tied to your margins.

Performance management

Meta and Google Ads: structure, audiences, creative testing and budget control.

Funnels and landing pages

From first click to booking or sale, with pages and forms built to convert.

Lead handling and follow-up

WhatsApp and CRM follow-up, status tracking and quality feedback from your sales team.

Reporting and monthly review

A monthly report that ends in decisions, goals and next month’s priorities.

Team and agency coordination

Briefing and managing designers, content creators, agencies and freelancers.

The monthly rhythm

  1. DiagnoseReview of your numbers, funnel and current activity.
  2. PlanA plan with goals, budget and priorities agreed with you.
  3. RunCampaigns, content and follow-up run against the plan, with regular check-ins.
  4. ReviewA monthly report and review that resets the plan for the next month.

How we work together

  • A monthly retainer with a written scope
  • Ad spend is separate from the retainer and paid by you to the platforms
  • Your ad accounts and data stay under your ownership

This is for you if

  • You have a marketing budget and an owner or manager who decides
  • You want one accountable lead instead of separate vendors
  • You are ready to share your numbers: ad accounts, sales and lead quality

It is not for you if

  • You need only design or posting without strategy
  • You want guaranteed results or a fixed number of leads

Questions

How is it priced?

The retainer depends on scope and the size of your marketing operation. I share it in writing after we review your numbers.

What do I need to start?

Access to your ad accounts and analytics, your sales numbers, and one person who can make decisions.

Do you replace my team?

No. I lead and coordinate your team, agency or freelancers, or build the plan they run.

Do you guarantee results?

No. I do not promise results or lead numbers. I manage against agreed goals and report honestly on what moved the business.
