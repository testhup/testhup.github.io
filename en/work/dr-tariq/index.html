Healthcare · Lead Generation

Dr. Tariq Hatamleh: turning reach into WhatsApp conversations

Two monthly reports (May and July 2026) for a surgeon’s Instagram, Facebook and Meta Ads, with WhatsApp conversations as the main result.

Sector
ENT & rhinoplasty surgeon
Market
Jordan
Reporting period
May and June–July 2026
Channels
InstagramFacebookMeta AdsWhatsApp
Source
Monthly performance reports

Business context

Dr. Tariq Hatamleh is a surgeon in northern Jordan. In July 2026 his accounts stood at 17.7K Instagram and 9.5K Facebook followers.

Healthcare: aesthetic and ENT surgery (rhinoplasty, children’s tonsils)

The challenge

Turn a large, growing audience into booking conversations for two procedure lines with different audiences.

After an exceptional June, July showed strong reach but weaker interactions and link clicks, which the report reads as creative fatigue after weeks of the same formats. Only 59% of the $600 ad budget was spent.

Strategic thinking

Treat the WhatsApp conversation as the main result, run separate campaigns per procedure and area, and use cheap reach and engagement campaigns to feed retargeting.

  • Keep an 80/20 content and budget focus between nose aesthetics and tonsils
  • Judge campaigns by cost per conversation, not by reach
  • Stop campaigns whose cost per conversation stays high
  • Rebuild engagement with real patient feedback and behind-the-scenes content

My role

Marketing manager on the account: responsible for the content and campaign approach, the Meta campaigns and the monthly performance report.

Execution

  • Monthly report with goals vs achieved for Instagram, Facebook and ads
  • Geographic split for tonsils: Irbid, Jerash and Mafraq
  • Profile-visit campaigns at $0.02–$0.03 per visit to build retargeting audiences
  • Pausing the two weakest campaigns ($1.10 and $1.25 per conversation)

Campaign structure

  • Message campaigns: nose aesthetics
  • Message campaigns: children’s tonsils, by area
  • Engagement and profile-visit campaigns
  • Retargeting recommended for people who messaged but did not book

Creative direction

  • Educational content, before/after and a podcast format
  • A six-reel series (“Dana”) planned for June
  • Real patient feedback in motion graphics without showing faces, planned for August

Business impact

346WhatsApp conversations in May 2026, from $209.68 of ad spend

245 · $0.61Conversations and cost each, same campaign line (July)
135 · $0.41Children’s tonsils campaign (July)
287.6KInstagram views in July (+14.7%)

May 2026: 346 WhatsApp conversations for $209.68 of ad spend across four campaigns. July 2026: 245 conversations at $0.61 (nose aesthetics) and 135 at $0.41 (tonsils), and Instagram reach at 221% of the month’s goal. Interactions and link clicks fell in July; the report flags this for the next month.

From the reports

Lessons & insights

  • Reach and conversations are different goals; track both
  • Creative fatigue shows up as falling interactions before it shows in reach
  • Under-spending the budget caps volume however good the cost per result
