Bond Dental Clinic: better leads, not just more leads

A lead-quality approach for a dental clinic: qualify earlier, so the front desk talks to people who are ready to book.

Sector
Dental clinic
Market
Egypt
Strategic concept. No performance report is attached to this project, so no results are shown. It describes how I would structure the work.

Business context

Bond Dental Clinic is a dental clinic in Egypt. Every booking starts with a conversation: a person sees an ad, messages the clinic and decides whether to trust it.

Healthcare: dental clinic, appointment-based

The challenge

Clinics rarely lack inquiries. They lack inquiries that turn into booked, paid appointments. A busy inbox can hide a weak pipeline.

Campaigns optimized for the cheapest inquiry attract price-shoppers and curiosity clicks. The front desk spends its time on people who never book.

Strategic thinking

Treat the booking, not the inquiry, as the goal. Work backwards from the appointment: what does a ready patient look like, and what would make the others self-select out?

  • Define the ideal patient by service and by intent, not only by demographics
  • Separate awareness, consideration and booking messages
  • Add qualifying questions before the conversation starts
  • Agree with the clinic on what counts as a qualified lead

My role

Strategy concept written by Karma Shawky. It shows how I would approach the project; it is not a report of delivered results.

Execution

  • Week 1: audit current ads, landing page and inquiry handling
  • Week 2: rebuild audiences and message angles per service
  • Week 3: launch the qualifying landing page and form
  • Ongoing: weekly review of lead quality with the front desk

Campaign structure

  • Prospecting campaigns by service line
  • Retargeting for people who viewed but did not book
  • Offer campaigns kept separate so promotions do not distort results

Creative direction

  • Plain, reassuring language over hard-sell claims
  • Real clinic context: team, space, process
  • Clear next step on every ad: what happens after you message

What this approach is designed to change

This approach is designed to give the front desk fewer, warmer conversations and to give the clinic a clearer read on which services and messages bring bookings. No performance figures are published here.

Lessons & insights

  • Cost per inquiry is a weak signal on its own
  • The front desk is part of the marketing system
  • Honest ads filter better than clever ones
