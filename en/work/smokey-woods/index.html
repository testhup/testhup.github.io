Smokey Woods: giving a restaurant a face the city remembers

Client overview

Smokey Woods is a restaurant in Irbid, Jordan. The June 2026 report covers its first full month of structured content and campaigns.

Industry

Restaurants and food service

Business challenge

Build recognition for a restaurant account in a city where most restaurant pages look alike.

Marketing problem

Menu-only content gives people no reason to remember or follow a restaurant, and campaigns that stop early cannot build frequency.

Strategic approach

Build a recurring character, “Abu Zaid”, around the food, then use paid campaigns to put that character in front of a new audience and to start conversations.

Marketing strategy

  • One recognisable personality across reels
  • Traffic campaigns for awareness, message campaigns for orders
  • Healthy frequency so the audience is not exhausted
  • Move to kitchen and behind-the-scenes content to turn followers into customers

Execution plan

  • Reels featuring Abu Zaid
  • Five paid campaigns launched between 23 June and 3 July
  • Weekly read of reach, frequency and link clicks
  • July plan: five kitchen reels, five new Abu Zaid reels, eight product and interactive posts

Campaign structure

  • Traffic: “From Abu Zaid” and “Wajid only”
  • Messages: three campaigns, including a meat-focused Thursday and Friday offer

Creative direction

  • A character-led voice instead of menu posts
  • Real kitchen footage to build trust
  • Product, wisdom and interactive posts to build the page’s visual identity

Solution

A character-led content line plus goal-based campaigns, read weekly against a target for each metric.

Business impact

June 2026: 184.8K Instagram views, 65.9K reach and 3.7K interactions; Facebook ended 54% above its monthly goal, and link clicks rose 332%. The report notes that all five campaigns were paused by the end of the period and names continuity as the one mistake to fix.

Key learnings

  • A character gives a restaurant something to be remembered for
  • Ads that stop early waste the awareness they started
  • Frequency below 2 left room to scale
