Veralex: following the ad spend all the way to confirmed sales

A July 2026 ads report for a Saudi company-formation service that connects spend, leads and confirmed sales in one sheet.

Business context

Veralex offers company formation, licensing and government procedures in Saudi Arabia, alongside Amazon and digital growth services. The ads in this report promote company formation to Arabic-speaking investors.

July 2026: EGP 10,368.77 spent, 109 leads and conversations, 2 confirmed sales and SAR 41,000 in revenue. Both sales came from the sales campaign, which cost EGP 3,704.92 for 31 conversations. The engagement video produced the most conversations (70) and no sales, and the two website-lead campaigns cost EGP 265–396 per lead. ROAS is not stated here because spend is in EGP and revenue in SAR.

From the reports

The challenge

A high-ticket service where one confirmed client matters more than a hundred inquiries.

Platforms report leads and conversations, not sales, so the cheapest result can hide the campaign that actually earns.

Strategic thinking

Record every campaign’s spend, leads, confirmed sales and revenue in one workbook so decisions rest on sales, not on cost per lead alone.

  • Test different objectives side by side: engagement, sales messages and website leads
  • Write each ad around one clear promise, such as full foreign ownership or registration in weeks
  • Compare lead-to-sale conversion by campaign
  • Spend on what sells, not on what is cheapest

My role

Marketing manager: responsible for the ad structure and the sheet that follows each campaign from spend to confirmed sale, and for reporting the results.

Execution

  • Four campaigns launched in July, mostly video creative
  • Weekly capture of spend, reach, frequency and link clicks
  • Sales confirmed with the team and entered against the campaign that produced them
  • A funnel sheet showing cost per lead, lead-to-sale rate and revenue per sale

Campaign structure

  • Sales campaign: three ways to set up a company, WhatsApp messages
  • Engagement campaign: setting up an online store in Saudi Arabia, video
  • Two website-lead campaigns: foreign ownership, and registration within weeks

Creative direction

  • Short videos, each built on one promise
  • Direct headlines: full foreign ownership, clear pricing from the first consultation
  • Calls to action matched to the objective: WhatsApp message, learn more, get a quote

Lessons & insights

  • Volume of conversations did not predict sales
  • The cheapest lead was not the one that paid
  • Two sales is a small sample: the next month should confirm the pattern
