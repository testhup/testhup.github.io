Customer Acquisition

Tres Jolie: an acquisition plan for a medical center

A customer-acquisition approach for a multi-service medical center: one service, one audience, one message at a time.

IndustryMedical center
My roleMarketing Manager
MarketUAE
ServicesCustomer AcquisitionMeta AdsContent StrategyBrand Positioning
These case studies describe the problem, the strategy and the way I structure the work. They are not performance reports, and no results figures are published here.

Client overview

Tres Jolie Medical Center is a medical center in the UAE offering several services. For a center like this, the hard part is not visibility, it is deciding what to promote and to whom.

Industry

Healthcare: medical center, multiple services

Business challenge

Promoting every service at once dilutes the message. Patients choose a center for one need first, then discover the rest.

Marketing problem

A broad, generic presence competes on price and proximity. Without a clear lead service per audience, ad spend spreads thin and results are hard to read.

Strategic approach

Pick entry services, build one acquisition path for each, and let patients expand into other services after the first visit.

Marketing strategy

  • Rank services by demand, margin and repeat-visit potential
  • Choose a small set of entry services
  • Position the center on trust and clarity, not discounts
  • Plan the second visit: what a new patient is offered next

Execution plan

  • Define entry services and target audiences
  • Write one message set per service
  • Launch Meta campaigns per service with a small testing budget split
  • Publish content that answers real patient questions
  • Review which services bring repeat visits

Campaign structure

  • Entry-service campaigns, one per service
  • Education content campaign to build trust
  • Retargeting for engaged viewers
  • Returning-patient reminders

Creative direction

  • Calm, clean visuals that fit a medical setting
  • Educational short videos and carousels
  • Careful, compliant wording for medical services

Solution

A service-led acquisition plan: a few entry services, each with its own audience, message and path, and a clear plan for bringing patients back.

Business impact

This approach is designed to focus spend, make results readable per service and build repeat visits. No performance figures are published here.

Key learnings

  • A center grows through a few clear doors, not all of them
  • Education builds the trust that ads cannot
  • Plan the second visit before the first
