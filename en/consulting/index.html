How it works

  1. Send a short brief
  2. We agree scope and fee in writing
  3. We do the work: audit, session or project
  4. You receive written findings and next steps

This is for you if

  • You spend, or plan to spend, on marketing in Egypt or the GCC
  • You want an honest outside view before spending more
  • You can act on recommendations yourself or with your team

It is not for you if

  • You want guaranteed results or a fixed number of leads
  • You only need someone to run a plan you already have (see Marketing Management)

Questions

How is consulting priced?

Fees depend on the format and scope. I share the fee in writing after your brief and before any work starts.

Which markets do you work in?

Egypt, Saudi Arabia and the wider GCC, in Arabic and English.

Do you guarantee results?

No. I do not promise results or lead numbers. I give recommendations based on your data and your market, and I am clear about what the data can and cannot tell us.

Can consulting lead to monthly management?

Yes, if it makes sense after the work. Marketing Management covers ongoing execution and oversight.
