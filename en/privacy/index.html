Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

What I collect

When you send a form, I receive what you type: name, email, WhatsApp number, company, country, your choices and your message. I also record the page you sent it from, your language, and how you reached the site (for example the campaign or link you came from).

Why I collect it

To reply to your request, to manage it, and to understand which channels and campaigns bring real inquiries.

Analytics

Analytics cookies are used only if you accept them. If you decline, the site still works.

Who can see it

Me, and the tools I use to receive and store inquiries, such as a form service, email and spreadsheets. I do not sell your information.

Your choices

You can ask me to show, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing me.
