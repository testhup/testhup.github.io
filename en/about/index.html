About Karma

Marketing is a business problem before it is a creative one.

I started in marketing in 2018 and moved through performance marketing, media buying and strategy. Along the way I've held Marketing Specialist and Marketing Manager roles for healthcare, training and service brands.

I work with clients in Egypt and the Gulf, and I'm used to markets where the same offer needs a different tone in Cairo, Riyadh and Dubai.

My approach is Profit First: I look at margins, lead quality and the sales conversation before I touch a campaign. A cheap lead that never buys is not cheap.

How I think
