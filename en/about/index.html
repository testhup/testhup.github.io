About Karma

Marketing is a business problem before it is a creative one.

I am a growth and digital marketing consultant, and a marketing manager who works as a business growth partner. I have been in marketing since 2018, across performance marketing, strategy and team leadership.

My clients are in Egypt, Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Jordan, mostly in healthcare, training, restaurants, real estate and services. I am used to markets where the same offer needs a different message in Cairo, Riyadh and Dubai.

I start with the business, not the ad account: margins, offer, lead quality and the sales conversation. A cheap lead that never buys is not cheap.

I work in two ways. In paid consulting you get an independent view and a plan. In monthly marketing management I lead the execution against goals we agree.
