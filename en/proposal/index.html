For projects that do not fit a single package: a growth partnership, a new-market launch, a marketing rebuild, or monthly management with a custom scope.

Request a custom proposal

Typical situations

  • You are launching or entering a new market, for example Saudi Arabia or the GCC
  • You want a marketing lead on a monthly basis, with a defined scope
  • Your marketing needs a rebuild: strategy, funnel and reporting
  • You have an agency or a team and need someone to lead and align them

How it works

  1. You send the brief
  2. We have a short call
  3. I send a written proposal: scope, deliverables, timeline and fee
  4. We agree and start

Questions

What should the brief include?

Your business, your goal, your current marketing, and a budget range if you have one.

How soon do I get the proposal?

After a short call I send the written proposal. I confirm timing on the call.

Do you guarantee results?

No. I propose goals and a way to measure them, and I report honestly against them.
